      How this connection works
      
            This Companion connects to Acadia Moodle. Your Moodle password and browser session stay on this computer account. Visible course text goes to your private Neyvu vault. The app never submits assignments or sends messages.
            
      
            Leave the app and its Moodle window open for updates every twenty minutes. Closing either window or sleeping the laptop pauses collection. Your last saved material remains available.
            
      
            This beta collects visible course and activity text, including linked forum discussions and book chapters. Each course check reads up to 100 activity, discussion and chapter pages, including up to 40 discussions, 10 forum index pages and 40 book chapters. Supported page, folder and book links can be followed two activity levels deeper. Verified Moodle PDF and text links can import originals, with up to 10 file attempts per course and 20 per refresh, a 10 MiB file limit and a 100 MiB refresh limit. PDF text extraction continues separately; check Sources for its current status.
            
      
            Saved counts show collection progress. Readable text can be searched and cited; it does not show that the AI understands the whole course. Hidden or unloaded content, content beyond the limits, unsupported or external files and media, grades and submission receipts remain limitations. Scanned or encrypted originals may need a readable text copy. Named collection gaps report problems encountered; compare the saved text and originals with Moodle.
